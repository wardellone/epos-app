# Privacy

Seller and contact: Federico — federico.neffa@gmail.com

Epos does not send audio or transcripts. Dictation runs on your PC and audio
stays in memory. Settings/vocabulary/corrections stay in `%APPDATA%\EPOS`;
logs in `.tmp`. New logs omit text unless you explicitly enable diagnostics;
legacy logs may contain it and can be removed in Info. During delivery, the
transcript briefly occupies the clipboard before supported prior content is
restored. If that content cannot be preserved safely, Epos leaves the clipboard
unchanged and uses direct text input. Windows or other programs may synchronise
the clipboard through their settings.

Model downloads (Hugging Face) and update checks (GitHub, once at startup and
on request) send connection data including IP addresses to those providers.
Their own privacy policies apply. There is no telemetry, account or online
licence check. For purchases the seller keeps only the name, email and payment
date needed to share the download folder and handle refunds. Payments go
through Revolut or the seller's bank under their own privacy policies. Do not
post logs or payment details publicly.
